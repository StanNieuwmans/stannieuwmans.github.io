Work that carries on without you.

Goals and Routines keep going in the background, inside limits you set. For code and the web, the Assistant has a Workspace of its own.

Goals

An objective over days
  1. 1 You say what you want. It proposes a Goal with a budget.
  2. 2 You press Start. Nothing runs before that.
  3. 3 It works in the background, a step an hour.
  4. 4 It checks in at least once per interval.

At the daily cap it pauses until tomorrow; at the total cap it stops. The Stop command halts every Goal at once.

Routines

Something on a schedule
  • Morning brief. Your meetings, mail that needs you, and what is waiting. Off until you switch it on.
  • Your own. Ask for “check the padel courts every Tuesday”. It proposes, you press Start.
  • Quiet hours. Times when it starts nothing at all.

Your own Routines are capped at €0.50 a run and €5 a month unless you say otherwise.

Workspace

Where it runs code and browses
  • Kept apart. It holds no credentials and cannot reach your Memory.
  • Asks first. A run that wants the web asks you before it starts.
  • Short-lived. Each run is limited in time and memory, and emptied afterwards.

On Railway it is a Railway Sandbox: a small VM, firewalled and tested before every run, and removed after 10 minutes unused.

Spend is counted from the tokens your provider reports, at the model’s price. For now those prices are provisional.

You decide what it may do alone.

Every action of every app has an Action tier. An Ask-first request expires after three days and never runs late.

  • Free Just does it, like reading your mail.
  • Notify Does it, then tells you, like saving a draft.
  • Ask first Asks each time, like sending a mail. Approve, edit or deny.
  • Never Off limits, and that cannot be changed.

What you need: a host, a domain name (Railway gives you one) and a model provider’s API key, unless you run a model yourself. Baken itself costs nothing.

Yours to take along: an Export holds every Memory, Chat, Goal, Routine and setting in open formats, locked with your passphrase, ready for a fresh Instance.

Make it an app.

Open your Instance in a browser and sign in with your passkey. Then put it one tap away.

iPhone

In Safari
  1. 1 Tap Share, the square with an arrow.
  2. 2 Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Open Baken from your home screen.

Android

In Chrome and others
  1. 1 Open the browser menu, the three dots.
  2. 2 Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app.
  3. 3 Open Baken from your home screen.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge
  1. 1 Open your Instance.
  2. 2 Choose Install.
  3. 3 Open Baken like any other app.

Safari on a Mac: File, then Add to Dock.

On an iPhone, notifications only work from the home screen. A desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux is in the works, with no download yet.
